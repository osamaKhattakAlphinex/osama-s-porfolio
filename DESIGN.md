# Design

Developer portfolio for two audiences, freelance clients and hiring teams. Light-first with a matching dark theme, typographic, one cobalt accent.
Source of truth: the "Osama Khattak Portfolio Redesign" canvas (every page and template was built from it).

## Tokens (src/app/globals.css)

- Ground and ink: paper `#f4f5f7`, white surfaces, ink `#0c1222`, muted `#4a5468`, rules `#dce0e7` / `#c9cfd9`.
- One accent: cobalt `#2d46e0` (hover `#1b2fa8`) for buttons, links and marks; `--accent-soft` `#e8ebfc` for tints and tags.
- Night surface: navy `#0c1222` for the featured bands (Staffvertex, process, proof, engineering notes) and the footer.
- Availability pill is the only warm colour (amber dot on `#fff4e2`).
- Dark theme keeps every role at lower luminance (`#080c16` ground, `#4760f5` buttons, `#9db0ff` link text). It follows the OS until the visitor toggles; the choice is stored in `localStorage.theme` and applied before paint by the inline script in `layout.tsx`. Without JS, `prefers-color-scheme` decides.
- Type: Bricolage Grotesque for headings (`.display`, `.heading`, `font-display`), Instrument Sans for body, JetBrains Mono for small uppercase labels (`.eyebrow`, dates, breadcrumbs, tags).
- Shape: 10-12px buttons and inputs, 14-22px cards and panels, 24-28px hero panels and the cobalt CTA band.
- Width: `max-w-[1200px]` container, 16px gutters on phones, 24px from 640px.

## Imagery and backgrounds (src/components/backdrops.tsx, src/components/icons.tsx)

- Only real imagery: project screenshots, the portrait and official tech logos. No stock photos or invented visuals.
- `Backdrop`: a faded line grid or dot field plus slow-drifting cobalt glows behind hero and intro sections.
- `ShotBackdrop`: a blurred, dimmed project screenshot with a grid behind the navy bands.
- `ScreenFan` (Work and Services intros), `ProjectReel` (About), screenshot headers on service cards, and the browser-plus-phone visual on each service page.
- Phosphor duotone icons in cobalt tiles (`IconTile`) mark services, process steps and skill groups.
- All of it is decorative (`aria-hidden`), sits behind content with `relative isolate`, and stops moving under reduced motion.

## Mobile

- The root font size is 87.5% under 640px and 93.75% up to 1024px, so type, spacing and buttons all shrink together on phones. Form fields stay at 16px so iOS doesn't zoom.
- Grids collapse to one column; the header nav becomes a menu under 860px; the article table of contents collapses into a disclosure.

## Motion

- `.rise`: load-in for the first screen, staggered by `--i`. H1s only slide (no fade) to protect LCP.
- `.reveal`: sections rise as they enter (CSS scroll-driven animation where supported).
- Screenshot cards lift on hover; the availability dot pulses in the home hero only.
- All of it is off under `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Rules

- One contact label everywhere: "Let's work together". Work links say "Read the case study".
- No invented numbers, clients or testimonials: every figure comes from `src/content/site.ts`. Where a fact is missing, leave it out rather than show a placeholder.
- Résumé buttons read "Request résumé" (mailto) until `person.resume` points at a PDF in /public; then they become downloads.
- Blog covers are generated by `node scripts/blog-covers.mjs` and carry no text, so the 21:9 crop never cuts a headline.
